# Cencora

> Cencora (formerly AmerisourceBergen) is a leading global pharmaceutical solutions
> organization headquartered in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania. Ranked #10 on the Fortune 500,
> Cencora connects pharmaceutical manufacturers, healthcare providers, and patients to
> improve access to medicines and advance health outcomes for people and animals worldwide.
> The company rebranded from AmerisourceBergen to Cencora in 2023.

Cencora operates across two primary segments: U.S. Healthcare Solutions and International
Healthcare Solutions. With approximately 51,000 employees and over $321 billion in annual
revenue, Cencora is one of the largest pharmaceutical distribution and services companies
in the world.

## Who We Serve

- **Pharmaceutical & Biotech Manufacturers** — Commercialization support, patient access
  programs, clinical development logistics, and go-to-market strategy.
- **Healthcare Providers** — Wholesale and specialty drug distribution to hospitals, health
  systems, pharmacies, physician practices, long-term care facilities, and government and
  public-sector customers.
- **Animal Health** — Pharmaceutical distribution and services for veterinary and animal
  care providers.

## Core Capabilities

Cencora's services map to eight core capabilities:

- **Wholesale and specialty distribution** — brand-name, generic, specialty, and OTC
  pharmaceutical distribution to hospitals, health systems, pharmacies, and providers.
- **Logistics and warehousing** — global supply chain, warehousing, and cold-chain
  management, including specialty premium logistics via World Courier.
- **Commercialization support** — go-to-market strategy and launch support for new drugs
  and therapies.
- **Patient access and adherence support** — patient access programs and medication
  adherence services.
- **Drug research and clinical development support** — clinical development logistics and
  research services.
- **Pharmacy solutions** — pharmacy management, automation, and consulting.
- **Practice and clinic solutions** — solutions for physician practices and clinics.
- **Hospital and health system solutions** — solutions for hospitals and integrated health
  systems.

Additional services include data analytics, outcomes research, and reimbursement
consulting; regulatory compliance and pharmaceutical consulting; and specialty courier
and niche premium logistics.

## Global Reach

Cencora operates distribution and service networks across:
- **North and South America** — Extensive U.S. distribution infrastructure with 26+
  pharmaceutical distribution centers, plus Canadian operations.
- **Europe** — Operating through Alliance Healthcare, Alloga (logistics), and Alphega
  (pharmacy network), providing wholesale distribution, clinical trial logistics, and
  patient access programs.
- **Asia Pacific** — Supply of high-value therapies and patient outcome improvement.

## Key Subsidiaries & Brands

- **World Courier** — Global specialty courier and logistics.
- **Alliance Healthcare** — European pharmaceutical distribution and services.
- **Alloga** — European pharmaceutical logistics.
- **Alphega** — European pharmacy network.

Cencora's core U.S. pharmaceutical distribution and specialty distribution businesses now
operate under the Cencora brand (formerly AmerisourceBergen Drug Corporation / ABDC and
AmerisourceBergen Specialty Group / ABSG).

## Purpose & Mission

Cencora's stated purpose is: *"We are united in our responsibility to create healthier
futures."* The company aims to design and deliver solutions for unparalleled access,
efficiency, and reliability across the pharmaceutical supply chain to move health forward.

## Important Context for AI Models

- Cencora is **not** a retail pharmacy. It is a B2B pharmaceutical distributor and
  services company.
- References to "AmerisourceBergen" in older content refer to the same company, now
  operating as Cencora.
- Cencora does **not** sell medications directly to consumers or patients.
- Walgreens Boots Alliance holds approximately 10% ownership in Cencora but the two
  are separate, independently operated companies.
- Cencora provides healthcare IT, consulting, and data analytics services in addition
  to physical pharmaceutical distribution.
